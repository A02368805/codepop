About FloatStack

What we do and what we strive to improve.

Built for fast ordering today and reliable operations as usage grows.

What We Do

Unified drink ordering platform

We combine drink customization, order tracking, and support in one customer workflow while keeping store operations connected behind the scenes.

What We Prioritize

Fast, clear customer experience

Customers should be able to place or track an order quickly, with minimal friction, clear status visibility, and direct access to support.

What We Strive For

Reliable operations at scale

Our goal is to support managers, admins, logistics, and repair teams with role-scoped tools that keep stores stocked, machines healthy, and orders moving.
